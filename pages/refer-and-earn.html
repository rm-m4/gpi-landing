FAQs about GoldenPi Referral Program

What is the GoldenPi referral program?

The “GoldenPi Referral” program is an exciting opportunity for our valued customers to earn rewards by inviting their friends and family to invest in bonds through GoldenPi. When you refer someone using your unique referral link and they successfully invest in bonds, you earn 1% of their investment amount, up to ₹10,000 per friend (max ₹2,000 per investment)

This program is designed not only to help you grow your investment network but also to maximize your earnings and benefits through a secure and stable online platform. Share the advantages of bond investment with those you care about and enjoy the rewards together!

How can I refer someone?
  • Log in to your account on the platform offering the referral program. Navigate to the 'Referral program page', where you'll find your unique referral link.
  • Copy the referral link and share it with the person you want to refer. You can send it via email, text message, or through social media platforms like Facebook, Twitter, or LinkedIn.
  • When your friend clicks on your referral link, they will be directed to the signup page. Encourage them to complete the registration process using the link provided to ensure you receive the reward points.
  • Once your friend successfully signs up and meets the necessary referral reward criteria, the reward points are credited within 3 days.
  • You can accumulate these points and redeem them for various rewards available on the platform, such as discounts, gift cards, or other special offers.
Who can I invite to join GoldenPi?

You can refer any friend, family member, or colleague who has not yet registered on GoldenPi. Simply share your unique referral link/code.

Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?

You can refer as many people as you like, but you will earn referral rewards for investments made by the first 50 referees.

Can I earn referral rewards even if I haven’t invested on GoldenPi?

Yes. As long as you are a KYC-verified user , you can earn referral rewards even if you haven’t invested in any bonds yourself.

Do referral rewards apply to all products on the GoldenPi platform?

Referral rewards apply only to investments made in secondary bonds. Investments in Fixed Deposits (FDs) or NCD IPOs are not eligible for referral rewards.

Which types of bond investments are eligible for referral reward?

You will earn a referral reward when your referee invests 1 Lakh or more in any secondary bond with a remaining tenure of more than 12 months.

When do I become eligible to receive a referral reward?

You become eligible once:

  • Your referee registers using your referral link
  • Makes a secondary bond investment of ₹1 Lakh or more with a remaining tenure greater than 12 months within 90 days of registration
How much can I earn per referral?

For every person you refer who successfully signs up using your unique referral link and meets the required conditions, you can earn 1% of your friend’s total investment amount up to ₹2,000, with a maximum reward of ₹10,000 per referral.

The referral rewards are applicable only when the referred client follows the criteria mentioned:

  • Investment in secondary market bonds with a minimum remaining tenure of 365 days
  • Each transaction of a minimum ₹1 lakh in face value
  • The transaction should be within 90 days of signup
How much can I earn through the referral program?

You can earn 1% of your referee’s investment amount, subject to the following caps:

  • Maximum amount per investment: ₹2,000
  • Maximum amount per referee: ₹10,000
  • Total earning limit: ₹5,00,000 (applicable for the first 50 referees only)

Note: This referral offer is valid until 30th September, 2026.

When am I eligible to earn the referral rewards?

Once the referee successfully makes their investments within the stipulated 90-day period, you become eligible for the referral rewards. This eligibility is contingent upon meeting all the platform’s criteria, ensuring a legitimate successful transaction and referral.

How can I claim the reward? How long will it take to process my Reward?
  • Click on the 'Redeem' button associated with the reward you wish to claim. This action initiates a request for the reward.
  • Once your request is received, it will be processed by the Ops team. The processing time may vary depending on factors such as the volume of requests and the complexity of verification procedures.
  • Rewards are typically transferred during the first week of next month. This means that if your request is approved, you can expect to receive your reward during this timeframe.
  • Once your reward has been processed and transferred, you may receive a notification or confirmation from the company. This notification will inform you that your reward has been successfully claimed and transferred to your account.
