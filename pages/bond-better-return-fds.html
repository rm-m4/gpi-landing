FAQs about investment in Bond IPOs India

What happens when I cancel my IPO application?

When you cancel your IPO application, all bids in the application will be canceled. However, you can apply again on or before the closing date. If you cancel the IPO application after funds are blocked by ASBA, your fund will be released within 48 hours of the allotment. In case the fund is not released, kindly contact the registrar of the NCD IPO issue for guidance.

When will I get the money back if I cancel?

When you cancel the IPO application after funds are blocked by ASBA, your fund will be released within 48 hours of the allotment. In case the fund is not released, you can write to contact-us@goldenpi.com for guidance.
