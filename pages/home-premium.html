Why bonds belong in your portfolio.

Bonds sit between the safety of a deposit and the growth of equity. You know what you will earn, and when, on the day you invest.

  • Predictable incomeInterest lands on fixed dates, monthly to yearly.
  • Higher than FDsRated corporate debt typically pays a premium over bank deposits.
  • Steadier than stocksReturns are set at purchase, not by daily market moods.
  • Real diversificationSpread across issuers, ratings and maturities.
