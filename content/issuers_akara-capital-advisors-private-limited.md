- History of Akara CapitalAkara Capital Advisors Private Limited (ACAPL) is a Delhi-based non-deposit-taking NBFC registered with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) since February 2016. The company commenced lending operations in 2017, primarily providing unsecured short-term personal loans to salaried individuals through web and mobile platforms under the brand Stashfin.ACAPL is a wholly owned subsidiary of Morus Technologies Pte. Ltd. (MTPL), a Singapore-based entity. MTPL owns two other subsidiaries: EQX Analytics Private Limited (EQXAPL), which operates the Stashfin technology platform and sources leads, and Titanium Fortune Financial Services Private Limited (currently non-operational). MTPL was founded by Mr. Tushar Aggarwal, who has over 20 years of experience in investment banking and private equity.

### Risks & considerations

- Bonds are safer than equities but not risk-free. Here's what to keep in mind before investing.

#### Credit risk

- If MFL's financial health deteriorates, principal & coupon payments could be delayed or impaired. Monitor rating actions by ICRA / CRISIL.

#### Concentration risk

- Avoid putting >10% of your debt portfolio into a single issuer. Diversify across NBFCs & PSUs.

#### Mitigating factors

- AA+ rating, secured charge on receivables, 1.25x asset coverage, low GNPA (3.85%), and CRAR well above the 15% regulatory floor.

#### Liquidity risk

- Secondary-market bid-ask spreads vary by series. Plan to hold to maturity for the published YTM.
