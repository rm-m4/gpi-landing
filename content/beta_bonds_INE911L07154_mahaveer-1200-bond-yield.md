- Mahaveer Finance India Limited (“MFL”) was incorporated on 5 Jan, 1981, is a CARE BBB+ rated company. It is a systematically important, non-deposit taking NBFC registered with RBI.
- MFL focuses on providing retail finance for used commercial vehicles. Its current focus mainly on the financing of used commercial and passenger vehicles. It has private equity investors including Banyan Tree Growth Capital, First Bridge India Growth Fund, and Elevation Capital.
- AUM grew from ~INR 1,007Cr in FY2025 to ~INR 1,324Cr in FY2026 with PAT of INR 30Cr. Company operates mainly across Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh, followed by Telangana, Karnataka, and Puducherry, supported by a network of 107 branches.

### Strength / Weaknesses

- Analysis

#### Key Strengths

- 01
- Experienced Promoters: Strong promoter pedigree with over 35 years of experience in vehicle financing, driving consistent growth through deep expertise in underwriting, collections, and customer relationships.
- 02
- Adequate Capitalisation: Strong capitalisation profile with over INR 295Cr of equity infused since FY2019, driving net worth to INR 418Cr, CRAR to 37%, and reducing gearing to 2.2x as of March 2026.
- 03
- Stable profitability: Stable profitability profile with ROAA consistently maintained at ~3% over the last six years, supported by healthy Net interest margins of ~11%, controlled credit costs below 1%, and PAT of INR 30Cr in FY2026.
- Risk Factors

#### Watch OUT

- 01
- Geographical risk: Geographic concentration remains high, with ~80% of AUM concentrated in Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh as of March 2026, exposing the portfolio to region-specific economic and regulatory risks.
- 02
- Vulnerable underlying customers: Customer profile remains relatively vulnerable, as the company primarily caters to first-time vehicle owners and small fleet operators, leading to moderately elevated but manageable delinquency levels.
